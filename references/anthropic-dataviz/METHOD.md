# Anthropic dataviz method (summary) — optional

Short form-first digest. **Not required** to emit a DataBook chart — prefer writing `src/charts/<Name>.tsx` and wiring `component` in `rows.json`.

**Rule:** color comes last. Pick the form from the data’s job (magnitude, identity, change-over-time, …) before picking a palette.

Anti-patterns: pie/donut for fine compares; 3D; dual axes when small multiples would do.

Upstream: Anthropic chart/dashboard design materials. Rights remain with Anthropic.
